# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Merchants managing services, availability, appointments, payments, and customer relationships; customers booking and managing services; authorized Calup staff investigating support and financial cases. Public marketing introduces the same product.

## Product Purpose

Help merchants publish bookable services and manage operations, and help customers confidently book, pay, and recover or manage their bookings.

## Brand Personality

Clear, dependable, efficient. Preserve Calup's established lime and black identity. Confirmed by the user on 2026-10-19 for the complete application review.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative clutter, inconsistent controls, ambiguous financial states, and visual effects that distract from completing a task. No specific external reference was supplied.

## Design Principles

- Make the next useful action apparent.
- Keep booking, payment, and recovery status explicit and trustworthy.
- Use consistent language and interaction patterns across surfaces.
- Prioritize mobile task completion and readable operational data.
- Preserve the established identity while removing unnecessary decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Assess against WCAG AA, including contrast, keyboard access, visible focus, programmatic labels, zoom/reflow, and reduced motion. This is an assessment target, not a claim of compliance.
